\title{Single simulation subject posterior prediction}
\author{B-TAJ}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

This follows the Tutorial simulation, then applies the single-subject
posterior-prediction idea used by \texttt{realdata\_pred}: the fitted
posterior is kept fixed, the selected subject's observed amyloid/tau
data are plugged in, and the subject's two random effects are estimated.
